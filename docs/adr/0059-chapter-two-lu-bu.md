## Context

Chapter 2 sits between the Yellow Turbans (chapter 1, ending in 192) and
관도 (chapter 3, from 199). It covers Cao Cao's hardest years: his father's
murder and the revenge war on Xuzhou, the revolt of his own province behind
Lü Bu, the emperor brought to Xu, the disaster at Wancheng, and the fall of
Lü Bu at Xiapi. Asked how many battles, the owner answered "everything":
the five recommended ones plus both six-battle extras.

## Options considered

1. **Seven battles, everything:** chosen by the owner.
2. **Five battles:** 서주, 복양, 정도, 완성, 하비 (recommended).
3. **Six, adding 장수 at 양성** or **six, adding 원술 at 진.**
4. **Four, with 서주 told in a scene.**

- **The chapter:** id `lubu`, floor Lv 10, starting from chapter 1's final roster; 29 steps (18 scenes, 7 battles, 4 camps).
- **Joins:**
  - 정욱 (194), 전위 (194), then 서황, 순유 and 곽가 (196).
  - 허저 (197).
  - At 백문루, 장료 or 여포 (ADR 0060).
- **Two choices** (서주, 백문루) and **one fate** (전위) branch the story (ADRs 0060, 0061).
- **New content:**
  - Twenty-four officers and six troop templates: 서주군, 여포군, 함진영, 장수군, 형주군, 원술군.
  - Two items: 방천화극 and 적토마.
- **Chain:** chapter 1 now hands on to chapter 2, and chapter 2 to 관도.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** the story runs without a gap from 174 to 200, and the chapter
  holds the period's best-known set pieces.
- **Negative / costs:** the longest chapter so far, about 1,500 lines of
  hand-written content, and two branch paths to balance and test.
- **Follow-ups:** balance numbers in STORY.md; playtest the two escapes.

## Revisit when

Playtests find the chapter too long between camps, or an escape battle confusing.
