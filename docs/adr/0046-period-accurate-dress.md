# 0046. Dress our characters as late Eastern Han soldiers and officials

- **Status:** Accepted; side colours swapped by [0055](0055-our-side-red.md) (ours red, the enemy blue)
- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Deciders:** project owner (asked for historical accuracy), Claude (details from period sources)
- **Related:** supersedes the class looks in [0045](0045-rigged-chibi-characters.md);
  [0002](0002-remake-from-scratch.md); [CHARACTER_LOOK.md](../remake/CHARACTER_LOOK.md)

## Context

The first painted figures ([0045](0045-rigged-chibi-characters.md)) borrowed from
fantasy and anime:
- round European helmets with plumes and crests,
- round shields with a boss,
- white plate armour with round chest discs (a style known from much later art),
- capes,
- ponytails and spiky hair.

The owner found them too far from the original games and asked for figures
grounded in real history: shields, clothing and the rest. Our basis is the
period itself, not the original games' art, which stays off limits
([0002](0002-remake-from-scratch.md)).

## Options considered

1. **Late Eastern Han material culture, around 200 AD:** chosen. The histories,
   Han texts on dress and archaeology give enough to dress every class.
2. **The popular image of the novel and later opera:** familiar, but full of
   later armour, flags and costume.
3. **Keep the stylised look:** no work, but the owner asked for accuracy.

## Decision

- **Everyone:**
  - Topknots under cloth caps or helmets; most grown men bearded.
  - Cross-collared robes, right over left, belted with a bronze hook.
  - Trousers, leg wraps and cloth shoes.
- **Soldiers:**
  - Iron lamellar cuirasses with shoulder flaps, laced in the side's colour.
  - Lamellar helmets with cheek and neck flaps and a tassel.
- **Infantry:** a ring-pommel sabre and a tall lacquered shield painted with a
  cloud scroll.
- **Cavalry:**
  - The ji halberd.
  - Officers wear two pheasant plumes after the Han martial cap.
  - Horses ride without stirrups, with saddle flaps, a bronze face ornament, a
    trimmed mane and a knotted tail.
- **Archers:** a cloth cap, a recurve bow and a hip quiver.
- **Strategists:** a silk headcloth, a long wide-sleeved robe and a feather fan.
- **Martial artists:** hemp work clothes and a bare topknot tied with cloth.
- **Stone-thrower:** a traction trebuchet on a cart with a banner, after the
  "thunderbolt carts" Cao Cao built at Guandu.
- **Colour:**
  - Side colours are Han dyes: indigo, madder red, green and ochre.
  - Dyeing robes by side is a game convention for readability, and the team
    ring stays.
- **Reference guide:** [CHARACTER_LOOK.md](../remake/CHARACTER_LOOK.md) lists
  each element, its basis and what to avoid. Artists follow it later.

## Consequences

- **Positive:**
  - The battlefield reads as the Three Kingdoms period, and the guide keeps
    future art consistent.
  - The Guandu stone-thrower ties the catapult class to our first scenario.
- **Negative / costs:**
  - Iron armour makes infantry and cavalry of both sides look alike from a
    distance; side colour now sits in the robe, lacing, tassel and ring.
  - Some choices rest on thin evidence; the guide marks them.
- **Follow-ups:**
  - A crossbow class.
  - Court dress (the 進賢冠 cap) for story scenes.

## Revisit when

A historian or the owner flags a detail, or an artist's research finds better
evidence for a part.
